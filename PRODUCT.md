# Product Context: Stitch Identity

## Users
Developers, SREs, and DevOps engineers managing authentication flows and security handshakes. They value speed, reliability, and "quiet" precision.

## Brand & Tone
Minimalist, high-performance, and technically deep. The tone is authoritative but understated. It should feel like a high-end terminal tool, not a consumer SaaS app.

## Strategic Principles
- **Clarity over Flash**: Use visual weight to guide the eye, not decorative gradients.
- **Precision**: Align everything to a strict grid.
- **Feedback-Driven**: Use color only for semantic feedback (success/error).

## Anti-References
- Generic SaaS "Magic" designs with colorful blobs and floating cards.
- Over-animated transitions that slow down the workflow.
