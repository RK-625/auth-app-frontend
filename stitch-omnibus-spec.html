This document merges deep technical UI specifications with our "Terminal-Chic" typography strategy. It acts as the single dictionary for building the Stitch Identity frontend.

Rules of Engagement

✓ DO

Use Space Grotesk for metrics and numbers to emphasize the technical nature of the app.

✓ DO

Use DM Sans for all form inputs, buttons, and paragraphs to prioritize extreme legibility.

✗ DON'T

Use Space Grotesk for paragraphs of text; it causes eye fatigue in long blocks.
